# Assessing Digital Skill Gaps Among Information Systems Undergraduates at Addis Ababa University: A Proposed Training Framework for Workplace Readiness

- **Course:** Research and Technical Writing (`INSY3061`), Section 2
- **Institution:** Addis Ababa University, College of Natural and Computational Sciences (CNCS), School of Information Systems
- **Instructor:** Dr. Ermias A.
- **Group:** Group 14

## Project Overview
This mixed-methods study investigates digital skill gaps and training needs among undergraduate Information Systems (IS) students at Addis Ababa University in relation to employer expectations and workplace readiness. It combines quantitative survey metrics (percentages and averages on tool confidence such as programming, collaboration platforms, version control systems, professional networking platforms, and data analysis tools) with qualitative instructor and student feedback to propose a practical training framework that prepares graduates for the workplace.

### Research Questions
- **RQ1:** What is the current level of digital skills (e.g., programming, collaboration platforms, version control systems, professional networking platforms, and data analysis tools) among Information Systems undergraduates at Addis Ababa University?
- **RQ2:** What training framework can be proposed to close the identified digital skill gaps and improve the workplace readiness of Information Systems undergraduates at Addis Ababa University?
